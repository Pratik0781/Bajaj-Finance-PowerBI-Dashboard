# 📊 Bajaj Finance Performance Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze sales, profit, COGS, units sold, products, segments, countries, and sales trends.

<img width="1170" height="663" alt="Bajaj Finance Dashboard" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a6e27dfd-4849-4e42-9adc-31c45142db11" />


## 🚀 Project Overview

The **Bajaj Finance Performance Dashboard** provides a comprehensive view of business performance using interactive visualizations and slicers.

The dashboard helps users quickly understand:

* Overall sales and profitability
* Sales trends over time
* Sales performance by customer segment
* Profit by segment and country
* Product-wise sales performance
* Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
* Units sold
* Year, country, segment, and product-level analysis

> **Note:** This project is created for data analytics and Power BI portfolio purposes. The dataset is used for analytical/dashboarding practice and should not be interpreted as official Bajaj Finance financial reporting.

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Trend Over Time

* Monthly/yearly sales trend analysis
* Identifies increases and decreases in sales
* Interactive filtering based on selected dimensions

### 2. Sales by Segment

Analyzes sales contribution across different business segments:

* Government
* Small Business
* Enterprise
* Midmarket
* Channel Partners

### 3. Profit by Segment

Compares profitability across different customer segments and helps identify the strongest-performing segments.

### 4. Sales by Product

Provides product-level sales analysis and highlights the highest-selling products.

Example products include:

* Paseo
* VTT
* Velo
* Amarilla
* Montana
* Carretera

### 5. Profit by Country

A geographical visualization showing profit distribution across different countries.

### 6. Interactive Filters & Slicers

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard using:

* **Year**
* **Country**
* **Segment**
* **Product**

A **Clear Slicers** option is also provided for resetting selections.
